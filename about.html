About

Hi, I’m Tricia.

I offer protective hairstyles in Vic and the Barcelona area: knotless braids, box braids, cornrows, twists, and other styles chosen with your hair in mind.

Appointments are by request, typically around three hours. The aim is a calm chair, neat parts, comfortable tension, and a style you can live in.

My approach

One guest at a time, with careful parting and even tension so the style feels as good as it looks.

The appointment

Plan for about three hours in a relaxed setting. Hours are 6:00 AM–6:00 PM, booked in advance.

Protective styling

Styles should look finished and still treat the hair and scalp with care from install through wear.

Request an appointment and we can find your next style together.
